Sleepmish Game Studio

An emerging two-person indie game studio. Every model and texture featured on this site is crafted from scratch by us in Blender — we do not use AI-generated imagery and strive to make everything by hand. We learn as we create, forging intriguing mechanics along the way. Currently, we are developing our debut title—a light horror experience set in Victorian England, brimming with intricate puzzles and a captivating atmosphere. We are at the very dawn of our journey and would deeply appreciate any support, even if it is simply a like on our social media.

Development Progress

The opening scene of our game is the protagonist's residence, which we are currently furnishing and tuning. Furthermore, we are actively implementing engaging mechanics for interacting with doors and environmental objects.
